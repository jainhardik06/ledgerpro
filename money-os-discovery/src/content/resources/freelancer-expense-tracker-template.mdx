## Freelancer Expense Tracker Template

Track every business expense with this production-ready template. Designed specifically for freelancers who need clean records for tax filing, client billing, and quarterly planning.

### What This Template Covers

- **Software & Subscriptions** — Track monthly and annual tools
- **Home Office** — Proportional rent, internet, utilities
- **Equipment** — Hardware, peripherals, depreciation schedules
- **Professional Development** — Courses, books, conferences
- **Marketing** — Website hosting, ads, design tools
- **Client Meals & Travel** — With receipt capture fields

### How to Use

1. Duplicate the template at the start of each fiscal year
2. Add a row for every business expense within 48 hours of purchase
3. Categorize using the provided category list (IRS-aligned)
4. Review monthly totals each month-end
5. Export the annual summary for your accountant
