This release makes Money OS genuinely usable for agencies and teams. **Multi-client tagging** lets you attach transactions to clients and flag reimbursable costs, and **team roles** mean you can give your accountant Viewer access without handing over the keys.

Every change is now captured in an immutable **audit log** — useful for teams and essential for trust.
