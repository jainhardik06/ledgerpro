## Step 1: Create Your Account

Navigate to [moneyos.webasthetic.in/register](https://moneyos.webasthetic.in/register).

Fill in:
- **Email address** — Use your primary business email. All financial exports and alerts will go here.
- **Password** — Minimum 12 characters. A password manager is strongly recommended.
- **Full name** — Used in audit logs and team notifications.

Confirm your email address by clicking the link in the verification email.

> **Note**: The verification email arrives within 60 seconds. Check your spam folder if it doesn't appear.

---

## Step 2: Name Your Workspace

After email confirmation, you'll be prompted to create your first workspace.

**Workspace name guidelines:**
- Use your business or brand name (e.g., `Acme Design Studio`)
- Keep it short — it appears in the top navigation bar
- Avoid special characters

**Workspace type** — Select the option that best describes your operation:
| Type | Best For |
|---|---|
| Freelancer | Solo operators, independent contractors |
| Agency | Teams billing multiple clients |
| Student Club | Campus organizations tracking dues and events |
| Small Business | Product businesses, local services |
| Startup | Funded or pre-revenue startups |

This setting affects which report templates and budget categories are pre-loaded.

---

## Step 3: Set Your Base Currency

Select the currency most of your transactions are denominated in.

> Money OS does not currently perform live currency conversions. Multi-currency support is on the roadmap.

---

## Step 4: Connect Your First Account

You can start with either:

**Option A — Manual entry**: Skip bank connection and add transactions manually. Good for freelancers who invoice irregularly.

**Option B — Import via CSV**: Upload a CSV export from your bank. Most banks offer this in Account → Statements → Download.

---

## Step 5: Invite Team Members (Optional)

If you're not working solo, go to **Settings → Team** and invite collaborators. Roles available:

| Role | Can See | Can Edit | Can Delete | Can Invite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | Everything | Everything | Everything | Yes |
| Member | Transactions, Budgets | Transactions, Budgets | No | No |
| Viewer | Everything | Nothing | Nothing | No |

New team members receive an invitation email and are prompted to create their own account.
