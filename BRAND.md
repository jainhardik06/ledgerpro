# Money OS Brand Guidelines (BRAND.md)

## 1. Identity & Perception
Money OS is a **Financial Command Center**. It must feel reliable, instantaneous, precise, and expensive. It should evoke the same trust as Stripe or Mercury.

- **Vibe**: Clinical, precise, quiet, and fast.
- **Audience**: Startup founders, agency owners, student club treasurers. They are busy. They do not want to be "delighted" by an accounting tool—they want answers immediately.

## 2. Voice & Tone (UX Writing)
- **Direct & Noun-First**: "Transactions" not "Your Transactions". "Settings" not "Manage Settings".
- **Actionable**: "Record Income" -> "Add transaction".
- **Zero Fluff**: 
  - Bad: "Here's what's happening with your money this month." (Current slop).
  - Good: "Overview" or "Current Month".
  - Bad: "Automate your fixed expenses at 0 cost."
  - Good: "Manage recurring payments."
- **No Exclamation Points**: "Transaction added!" -> "Transaction added."

## 3. Visual Identity
- **Logo**: Typographic. `Geist` font. `Money OS`. No massive, saturated, multi-color icons. Just a sharp, monochrome logomark.
- **Restraint**: We do not use color to decorate. We use color to convey status (Credit = Green, Debit = Red). Everything else is neutral.

## 4. Anti-Patterns to Remove
- "Unicorn Gradients" masking as premium design.
- Overly friendly, conversational empty states ("No expenses this month! Add one to get started!").
- Massive icons next to headers.
