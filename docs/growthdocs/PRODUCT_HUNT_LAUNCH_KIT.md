# Product Hunt Launch Kit

## Overview

Product Hunt launch is the single highest-impact day of earned distribution available to an indie product. A well-executed launch generates:
- 500-2,000 sign-ups in 24 hours (depending on final rank)
- High-DA backlink from producthunt.com (DA 91)
- Press and newsletter pickup from top PH finishes
- Community validation for investor conversations

A poorly-executed launch — wrong timing, wrong asset quality, insufficient upvotes in the first hour — will not rank, will not get coverage, and cannot be repeated for the same product.

**Do it once. Do it right.**

---

## Pre-Launch Timeline

### T-30 Days (30 Days Before Launch)

**Claim your product**
- Go to `producthunt.com/posts/new` (or ship a teaser page)
- Create "Upcoming" page to start collecting followers
- Fill: name, tagline, thumbnail, short description, topic tags

**Community building**
- Follow 50+ active Product Hunt users in the finance/productivity space
- Comment authentically on 3-5 PH posts per week
- Build relationships before you need anything — don't appear only on launch day

**Gather early supporters**
- Identify 50 people who will vote on launch day: friends, early users, founders in your network
- Do not ask generically on social — identify people who actually use or care about the product
- Prepare a direct message explaining what the product does and when you're launching

### T-14 Days (14 Days Before Launch)

**Asset Creation**

| Asset | Spec | Status |
|-------|------|--------|
| Thumbnail (logo) | 240×240 PNG | Create |
| Gallery Image 1 | 1270×760 PNG (dashboard) | Create |
| Gallery Image 2 | 1270×760 PNG (transactions) | Create |
| Gallery Image 3 | 1270×760 PNG (reports) | Create |
| Gallery Image 4 | 1270×760 PNG (budget view) | Create |
| Demo video | 60-90 seconds, no voiceover | Optional |
| Maker tagline | 60 chars max | Write |
| Product description | 260 chars max | Write |
| First comment | 500-1000 words, maker story | Write |

**Asset Quality Standards**
- Dark background (`#000000`) consistent with design system
- Real product screenshots — not mockups
- Text in images must be legible at thumbnail size
- No brand colors except Money OS's own palette

**Landing page update**
- Add "Featured on Product Hunt" badge placeholder to `moneyos.app`
- Ensure the homepage loads in <3 seconds on mobile
- Test sign-up flow — it must work flawlessly on launch day

### T-7 Days (One Week Before)

**Finalize launch date and time**
- Best launch day: **Tuesday or Wednesday**
- Worst launch day: Monday (competitive) and Friday/weekend (low traffic)
- Time: 12:01 AM PST on launch day (Product Hunt resets at midnight PST)
- Launching at 12:01 AM PST gives 24 hours to accumulate votes
- Launching at noon loses half the voting day

**Prepare outreach messages**

Draft messages for:
1. **Close friends and family**: Personal, direct. "I'm launching my product on Product Hunt on [DATE]. It would mean a lot if you checked it out: [URL]"
2. **Early users**: Warm, personal. "You've been using Money OS — thank you. I'm doing a formal launch on PH on [DATE]. A vote would go a long way."
3. **Maker network**: Founder-to-founder. "Launching Money OS on PH this [DAY]. Would appreciate your support — happy to return the favor on your next launch."
4. **Twitter/X announcement**: Public, with link.

**Do NOT**:
- Send bulk generic emails ("Hey everyone, vote for me!")
- Ask in communities where you've never contributed
- Offer incentives in exchange for upvotes (PH bans accounts for this)

### T-1 Day (Day Before Launch)

- [ ] All gallery images uploaded
- [ ] Maker tagline finalized (60 chars max)
- [ ] Product description finalized (260 chars max)
- [ ] First comment written and ready to paste
- [ ] Outreach messages drafted for each group
- [ ] Launch page scheduled for 12:01 AM PST tomorrow
- [ ] Notify your 50 launch supporters about the exact time

## Launch Day Execution

### Hour 1 (12:01 AM — 1:00 AM PST)

This is the most critical hour. PH's algorithm heavily weights early velocity.

**Sequence**:
1. **12:01 AM** — Submit launch (or it goes live automatically if scheduled)
2. **12:02 AM** — Post your first comment (the maker story — see template below)
3. **12:05 AM** — Send personal messages to your 10 closest supporters immediately
4. **12:15 AM** — Post to Twitter/X: "We're live on Product Hunt! [link]"
5. **12:30 AM** — Post in Indie Hackers: "Show IH — just launched on Product Hunt"
6. **1:00 AM** — Check position. Send second wave of outreach messages.

### Hours 2-12

- Send outreach to remaining supporters in waves of 10-20 every 2 hours
- Respond to every comment on your PH page within 30 minutes
- Do not ask for upvotes directly ("Please upvote") — ask for genuine feedback
- Post updates in comments: ship any small improvement and announce it

### Hours 12-24

- Final push: share in relevant communities
- Twitter/X reminder post
- Respond to all outstanding comments
- If ranking well (top 5): reach out to PH newsletters for coverage

## Maker Story — First Comment Template

```
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I built Money OS after spending 4 hours reconciling my freelance expenses in 
a spreadsheet the night before a tax filing deadline. It was December. I had 
client receipts in email, transactions scattered across two credit cards and 
a bank account, and invoices in HoneyBook — none of it talking to each other.

I looked for a tool that could:
→ Import any bank CSV without a bank connection requirement
→ Track expenses by client (for reimbursable billing)
→ Set budgets per category with alerts
→ Generate a summary my accountant could actually use

Everything I found was either $20+/month (overkill), required Plaid bank 
connections (not available in India), or had a UX designed in 2014.

So I built the version I wanted.

**What Money OS does today:**
• CSV import from any bank or payment processor
• 8 pre-configured freelancer expense categories
• Multi-client expense tagging with reimbursable flags
• Category budgets with 80%/100% email alerts
• Expense Summary, P&L, Cash Flow, and Client Reports
• Role-based team access (Admin / Member / Viewer)
• Full audit trail for every change

**Who it's for:**
Freelancers, agencies, small teams, and student clubs who want financial 
clarity without accounting software complexity.

**What's free:**
Everything above. No credit card. No artificial limits for the core use case.

**What's coming:**
Direct bank connections, approval workflows, and invoicing are on the roadmap.

I'd love your feedback — what would make this genuinely useful for your 
specific situation? The roadmap is shaped by these conversations.

[App link] | [Docs link]
```

## Post-Launch

### Day 2 — Capitalize

- Thank everyone who commented and voted
- Write a public post-mortem on Indie Hackers: "[X] votes, [Y] sign-ups — what worked on our Product Hunt launch"
- Add "Featured on Product Hunt" badge to homepage (PH provides the badge HTML)
- Share final rank in launch_metrics MongoDB collection

### Week 1 Post-Launch

- Follow up with every commenter who had a question or feature request
- Capture all feedback in the MongoDB `user_feedback` or `content_briefs` collection
- Identify any sign-ups who left within the first 24 hours — reach out for the reason

## Dos and Don'ts

### Do
- Launch on Tuesday or Wednesday
- Start at 12:01 AM PST
- Write a genuine, personal first comment
- Respond to every comment
- Send personal outreach (not mass email)
- Share real product screenshots
- Be honest about what's not built yet

### Don't
- Launch on a Monday (too competitive) or weekend (low traffic)
- Use mockups instead of real screenshots
- Incentivize upvotes (PH will ban the account)
- Ignore comments
- Relaunch the same product without significant updates
- Launch during a major news event
- Launch the same week as major competing products
